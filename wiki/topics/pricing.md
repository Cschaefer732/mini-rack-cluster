# Pricing

Full table in the repo `README.md`. Two columns: price paid (or original MSRP
for parts reused from the prior nova-linux build, where no receipt survives),
and current market price as of 2026-09-26.

## Key finding

Total paid/original ≈ $10,990. Total market now ≈ $11,519. **The build is
worth more today than it cost to assemble** — every reused component
(motherboard, CPU, both NVMe drives, RAM) appreciated due to the 2026
GPU/NAND/DRAM shortage, outpacing normal hardware depreciation. This is the
headline for the [[linkedin-post]].

## Uncertain figures (flagged, not fabricated)

- RTX 4070 Ti Gaming Trio and X670E ACE / 9950X: no purchase receipt exists
  (reused from an earlier nova-linux build) — MSRP used as a stand-in.
- Crucial 32GB DDR5-4800 launch price: no clean historical record found;
  ~$110 is an estimate based on typical 2023 DDR5-4800 32GB pricing, not a
  sourced figure.
- TRYX riser vs. ARCTIC AIO: both showed $74.99 on the same purchase date in
  screenshots — user did not confirm which paid figure belongs to which item,
  so both are listed as ~$74.99.
